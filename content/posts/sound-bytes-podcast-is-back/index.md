Well it has been a little over a three month since their original Podcast server bit the dust. But I am happy to welcome back the [Sound Bytes Podcast](http://soundbytes.org/archives/), and now I have two months of shows to catch up on. Thanks guys!

For anybody not familure with the Sound Byte Guys here is a quick biography of the program.

> Since 1989, Sound Bytes has been a call-in radio show on public radio. That airs every Sunday from 11 AM to 1 PM, on 1180 WHAM out of Rochester, NY bringing you news, views, and stuff you can use, as the guys talk about hardware, software, nifty toys, industry trends, silly stuff, and just about anything that flits through our brains.

You can listen to their show every week via [streaming audio](http://www.radio-locator.com/cgi-bin/url?bc=y&call=WHAM&band=AM&service=AM) out of [Rochester, NY](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester%2C_NY) or visiting their [website](http://soundbytes.org/).
