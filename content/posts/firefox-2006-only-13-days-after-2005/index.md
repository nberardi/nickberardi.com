Well it is official Firefox just [released version 2.0.0.6](http://en-us.www.mozilla.com/en-US/firefox/2.0.0.6/releasenotes/) which makes the old version 2.0.0.5 just 13 days old and not needed anymore. The following issues were fixed:

- [MFSA 2007-27](http://www.mozilla.org/security/announce/2007/mfsa2007-27.html) Unescaped URIs passed to external programs
- [MFSA 2007-26](http://www.mozilla.org/security/announce/2007/mfsa2007-26.html) Privilege escalation through chrome-loaded about:blank windows

This brings up the real question which is more annoying Windows Update (the passive update process) or Firefox update (the active update process). Honestly I prefer to stay ignorant about browser updates, because honestly I don't really care about unescaped uris. Not because I don't think they are a threat, but because I know the signs of a potential site that could cause harm and just avoid them.
