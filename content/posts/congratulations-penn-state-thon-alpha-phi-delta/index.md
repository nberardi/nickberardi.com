I would like to congratulate the [Penn State IFC/Panhellenic Dance Marathon (THON™)](http://www.thon.org/), it is the largest student-run philanthropy in the world, and this past weekend they raised **$6,615,318.04** for children with cancer. And a special congratulations to my fraternity [Alpha Phi Delta](http://apd.org/), Chi Chapter, for participating and helping to raise money for this great cause.
