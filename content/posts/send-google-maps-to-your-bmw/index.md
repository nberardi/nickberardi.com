Google Maps Germany has a new feature: if you have a BMW car that includes a navigation system and you happen to live in Germany, it's easy to send [the address of a local business](http://maps.google.de/maps?f=l&hl=en&q=hvb&near=koln&layer=&ie=UTF8&z=10&ll=51.111713,6.995544&spn=0.412974,1.2854&om=1&iwloc=A&iwd=1&cid=50920182,6934348,16790520539613261006) to your car's navigation system.

The "send" link from every Google Maps page will open a dialog that lets you fill your BMW account name and send an address plus some notes to your car. This service is free and it works only for businesses in Germany.
