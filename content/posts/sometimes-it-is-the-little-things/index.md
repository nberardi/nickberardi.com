About 2 weeks ago I wrote about [What I loved about Google Adsense](/posts/what-i-love-about-google-adsense/).  And today I just wanted to show you the results that I have received over the past two weeks with my little experiment.  Well it was an experiment on my part, because I didn’t know the outcome.  But to Google it was a pure measure of analytics, and I am happy they sent me the email.

![Estimated earnings](/images/2010/09/image.png "Estimated earnings")

![Page eCPM](/images/2010/09/image1.png "Page eCPM")

![Page CTR](/images/2010/09/image2.png "Page CTR")

I know most of you probably don’t speak the online marketing lingo, but basically the clicks through rate (CTR) per page has stayed the same, but my eCPM or the rate per ad clicked is now 350% of what it was just two weeks ago.  So again I say that I am glad Google is watching out for us AdSense content delivers.  Because it has a direct effect on my bank account, as well as their I imagine.
