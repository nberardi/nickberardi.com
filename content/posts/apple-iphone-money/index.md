Today Apple had their big [iPhone SDK press release](http://www.engadget.com/2008/03/06/live-from-apples-iphone-press-conference/). They opened up the phone and added a bunch of Enterprise features that many small to large corporate customers have been asking for. But they still haven't implemented the features I am looking for in a smart phone, and until they do, they will not see my money.

1. Microsoft Exchange Direct Push
2. [Tethering](http://www.coderjournal.com/2007/06/how-to-connect-to-cell-phone-internet-though-windows-vista/ "How To: Connect To Cell Phone Internet Though Windows Vista")
3. 3G Network (I currently use Verizon)

I know #2 will be coming, if not through Apple somebody else will do it, since Apple has unlocked the iPhone to developers.  However #3 is the one that I may have to [wait for Verizon to switch to GSM](http://www.engadget.com/2007/09/21/verizon-dumps-cdma-for-gsm-based-lte-in-4g-networks/) in the distant future.  Other than my last 2 requirements I am sold on the iPhone.
